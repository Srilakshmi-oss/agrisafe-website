ABOUT OUR VENTURE

Protecting the people who feed us

AgriSafe is a proposed IoT-based early warning system designed to detect snakes in agricultural areas and alert farmers through an alarm and mobile notifications. Our goal is to make farm work safer and give farmers greater peace of mind.
